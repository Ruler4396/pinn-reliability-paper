# Related Work Reference Shortlist

用于主文 related work 与最终参考文献整理。以下条目优先对应当前主文对比表中的最近邻工作。

## Foundational PINNs

1. Maziar Raissi, Paris Perdikaris, George Em Karniadakis.
   Physics-informed neural networks: A deep learning framework for solving forward and inverse problems involving nonlinear partial differential equations.
   Journal of Computational Physics, 378:686-707, 2019.
   arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10561

## Noisy Data / Bayesian / UQ

2. Liu Yang, Xuhui Meng, George Em Karniadakis.

   Journal of Computational Physics, 425:109913, 2021.
   arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.06097

3. Zongren Zou, Xuhui Meng, George Em Karniadakis.
   Uncertainty quantification for noisy inputs-outputs in physics-informed neural networks and neural operators.
   arXiv, 2023.
   arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.11262

4. Sifan Wang, Yujun Teng, Paris Perdikaris.

   SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing, 43(5), A3055-A3081, 2021.

5. Sifan Wang, Xinling Yu, Paris Perdikaris.

   Journal of Computational Physics, 449:110768, 2022.

   A comprehensive study of non-adaptive and residual-based adaptive sampling for physics-informed neural networks.
   Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering, 403:115671, 2023.
